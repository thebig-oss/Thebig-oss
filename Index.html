Happy Monthsary, My Love!

Dearest Love,

Happy Monthsary! I'm writing this letter through code because I wanted to make you something a little something for you, crafted just for you to remind you of how much you mean to me.

As another month goes by, I find myself sitting down to reflect on everything we share. Every single day with you is a gift, but marking another month together gives me a moment to step back and appreciate just how truly special you are in my life.

First of all, I want to sincerely apologize for our recent argument; I never want us to fight. It broke my heart that we had a disagreement, and I hate knowing that words or misunderstandings caused tension between us. I am so sorry for my part in it. Your peace of mind, your smile, and your happiness mean the absolute world to me, and the last thing I ever want is to cause you distress.

Relationships aren't always perfect, but passing through a rough spot together only proves how strong our connection is when we choose each other every day. I don't ever want to let pride or silly arguments get in the way of what we are building together.

I am also here to say that I'll always support you and love you through everything. Whatever dreams you are chasing, whatever goals you set, or whatever difficulties life throws your way, you will never have to face them alone. I am your biggest fan, your safe place, and your partner in everything. I believe in you even when you doubt yourself, and I will always stand proudly right by your side.

Let's make this our special, modern, monthsary tradition. Thank you for your patience, your kindness, and for giving me your heart. You're everything to me, and I'm looking forward to creating so many more beautiful memories together.

I love you more than words can express, today and forever.

Love always,
your only wife 💙
